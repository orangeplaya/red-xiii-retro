Structural template — the "growing up walk"

  The shot is essentially one continuous idea across three environment cuts,
  with a static side-on camera and characters always walking left-to-right in
  the same formation (large character center, medium-large left, small right).

  Segment 1 — frames 1–3 (t=188–190s) — Jungle, day
  - Background: lush green bokeh jungle, dappled sunlight
  - Ground: flat mossy rock ledge
  - Character state: young Simba cub, Pumbaa, Timon
  - Foreground: blurred tropical leaves frame the edges
  
  Segment 2 — frames 4–6 (t=191–193s) — Waterfall canyon, dusk
  - Hard cut to a new environment: warm orange/pink cliff walls, multiple tall
    cascading waterfalls
  - Ground changes to a mossy log over water — reflection visible below
  - Simba visibly bigger (adolescent), same character order
  - Light is warmer/cooler contrast — orange rock vs. blue-white water
  
  Segment 3 — frames 7–10 (t=194–198s) — Moonlit night
  - Large full moon rises centered in frame as a backlight disc
  - Sky deepens to full night blue 
  - Simba grows from adolescent → full adult lion with complete mane across
    just these 4 seconds
  - Silhouette-style lighting kicks in as moon dominates — characters go
    darker against the glow
  - Water reflection of moon below the log
  
  ---

  Key production elements to replicate:
  1. Side-on locked camera — no pan, characters scroll through frame
  2. Foreground bokeh plane — blurred vegetation/bark as a frame within a
     frame
  3. Horizontal stage — a single ledge/log the characters walk along at
     mid-frame
  4. Three-environment structure — each ~3s, each with distinct color palette
  5. Time-lapse growth — the main character visually ages across the cuts
  6. Moon as climax device — the large backlit moon in the final segment is
     the emotional peak
